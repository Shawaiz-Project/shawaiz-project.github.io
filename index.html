Lightweight 196,773-parameter student for binary COVID-19/Non-COVID CT classification, fusing a depthwise-separable CNN (Coordinate Attention) with a 134-D cubical persistent-homology descriptor (GUDHI) via reliability-aware gated fusion, distilled from a frozen EfficientNet-B0 teacher. Includes the full A0–A10 ablation suite, frozen split manifest, bootstrap CIs, and McNemar tests.

🎓 Research

Research

TopoLite-KD: Efficient Topology-Aware Knowledge Distillation for COVID-19 CT Slice Classification

Authors: Ibadat Ali · Shawaiz Ali · Muhammad Abdullah · Muhammad Usama

Department of Computer Science, GIFT University, Gujranwala, Pakistan — 2026

Binary CT classification recipe combining a depthwise-separable visual encoder, a fixed 134-D persistent-homology descriptor, reliability-aware gated fusion, and supervised response-based distillation from a frozen EfficientNet-B0 teacher. Frozen split, 12-config ablation suite (A0–A10), bootstrap CIs and paired McNemar tests.

Live Experiment on Kaggle GitHub
